Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Your audio never leaves your device. Every tool on StemSplit — the stem splitter, pitch shifter, BPM finder, cutter, joiner and speed changer — runs entirely in your browser. We never receive, store or see your files.

What we collect

  • Your audio files: nothing. They are decoded and processed locally in your browser tab and never transmitted to us.
  • Accounts: there are none, so there is nothing to store.
  • Contact messages: if you email us, we receive whatever you choose to include so we can reply.

Cookies & analytics

StemSplit does not set tracking cookies and does not run third-party analytics that follow you across the web. The site loads fonts and a zip utility from public CDNs, which may log standard technical data (like your IP address) according to those providers' own policies.

Microphone access

The Studio's record feature asks your browser for microphone permission. Recorded audio is processed locally exactly like an uploaded file and is never sent anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Because we collect essentially nothing, there's very little that could change — and we'd rather keep it that way.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the contact page.
