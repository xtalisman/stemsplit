About

Audio tools that respect you

StemSplit started with a simple frustration: every "free" audio tool wanted an upload, an account, and a subscription before doing anything useful.

What we believe

Your computer is already powerful enough to split stems, shift pitch, detect tempo and edit audio. There is no reason your songs should travel to a stranger's server — and wait in a queue — for work your own browser can do in seconds.

So we built StemSplit as a local-first toolkit: every tool runs entirely in your browser tab using the Web Audio API. No uploads, no accounts, no watermarks, no usage caps.

How the separation works

The Studio uses center-channel (mid/side) separation, a classic studio technique: in most stereo mixes the lead vocal sits dead center while instruments spread across the stereo field. We extract the center as the Vocals stem and the wide signal as the Music stem. Because the two stems add up to the original exactly, the Custom Mixer can rebuild any balance losslessly.

It's honest signal processing — not magic — which is why we show you the method and let you judge the results with your own ears before downloading anything.
