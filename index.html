How it works

From full song to separate stems
in three steps

No installs, no accounts, no waiting in a render queue.

01

Drop in your track

Upload an MP3, WAV, M4A or even a video file — or hit record and capture audio live. Your file is decoded instantly on your own device.

02

Split the stems

StemSplit pulls the recording apart into a Vocals stem and a Music stem using center-channel separation, tuned for real-world mixes.

03

Preview, mix & download

A/B each stem on a shared timeline, rebalance them in the Custom Mixer, then download individual WAVs or grab every stem as a zip.

FAQ

Questions, answered

StemSplit uses center-channel (mid/side) separation: in most stereo mixes the lead vocal sits in the center while instruments spread wide. We extract the center as the Vocals stem and the wide signal as the Music stem — entirely on your device, in seconds.

Yes — every tool is free with no account, no watermark and no download limits. Because all processing happens in your browser, there are no server costs to pass on to you.

No. Your audio never leaves your computer. Decoding, separation, analysis and rendering all run locally in your browser tab, and nothing is stored on any server.

MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and most video files (MP4, WebM, MOV) — the audio track is extracted automatically. Files up to 10 minutes long work best in the browser version.

Center-channel separation works beautifully on typical commercial mixes where the vocal is centered. Results vary with the mix: heavily processed, mono, or off-center vocals separate less cleanly. Try the Custom Mixer to find the blend that works for your track.

StemSplit doesn't change who owns the original recording. You may only use separated audio in ways your rights to the source material allow — for example, your own recordings, licensed tracks, or royalty-free music.

Yes — the Studio and all tools run in any modern mobile browser. For very long files, a desktop browser will feel snappier.

All downloads are 16-bit WAV at your file's original sample rate — lossless, ready for any DAW or video editor.
